02/05 · My creative perspective

I’m here to experiment.

I’m interested in how a simple idea can turn into something unexpected through graphic design, illustration, and experimentation.

I’m still developing my style, and that’s part of what excites me. I like exploring different directions, testing ideas, and learning from the process instead of expecting the first attempt to be the final one.

A single, simple thought. Nothing is decided yet.

03/05 · My vision

My creative community — Navarasa

Proposed concept · not an existing organisation

“Different people. Different ways of seeing. One community.”

The idea

I imagine Navarasa as a student-led creative community where different disciplines and perspectives come together.

From illustration and animation to film and photography.

Inspired by the idea of Navarasa in Indian aesthetics, it celebrates different ways of expression. It would be an experimental space where students can collaborate and learn.

This is my vision for a community I’d like to help build as a Campus Leader.

The core of it

  • Open to students from every department.
  • Collaborative projects over solo practice.
  • Experimental, low-pressure and beginner-friendly.

How it could work

Learn together→Create together→Share together↻

  1. 01 · Creative Workshops

    Learn together. Beginner-friendly sessions where students explore graphic design, illustration, animation, and other digital creative tools.

  2. 02 · Open Creative Challenges

    Create together. Short, approachable prompts that encourage students to make something without worrying about perfection.

    See the Creative Crunch example ↓

  3. 03 · Student Showcase

    Share together. A shared space, digital or physical, where students can present their work, discover other approaches, and exchange feedback.

What students see in the showcase sparks the next workshop. A proposed cycle for how Navarasa could function.

A sample initiative Navarasa could organise

Creative Crunch

One prompt. One day. Endless interpretations.

Proposed activity · conceptual example

Make the ordinary unexpected.

Students respond to the same creative prompt in their own way, using any medium they choose. The results can be shared in a collective showcase, creating an opportunity to discover different approaches, exchange ideas, and experiment without worrying about perfection.

  1. Drop the promptOne prompt is shared with everyone at the same moment.
  2. Start the clockLimited Time. Any tool, any medium, any skill level.
  3. Make somethingA poster, an illustration, a collage, or something nobody has thought of yet.
  4. Share itEverything goes into one shared space. No ranking, just more people making.

Three Conceptual Examples

No experience required. Just curiosity.

Open to beginners and experienced creators alike. Bring a skill you already have, try something unfamiliar, or collaborate with someone who sees things differently.

04/05 · My perspective on AI

AI helps me explore. I decide what works.

I use AI as part of my creative process to brainstorm ideas, explore visual possibilities, and experiment with directions I might not have considered.

But generating options is only the beginning. I still need to question the results, make choices, refine the design, and decide what communicates the idea best. For me, AI is most useful when it expands my thinking rather than replaces it.
